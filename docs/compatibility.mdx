# Compatibility

## Versions and loaders

| Minecraft | Loaders | KubeJS integration |
|---|---|---|
| 1.20.1 | Forge, Fabric | Forge and Fabric |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge, Fabric | NeoForge |
| 26.1.2 | NeoForge, Fabric | NeoForge |

On Fabric the config screen opens from **Mod Menu**. On Forge and NeoForge it opens from the built-in mods list. See [config](./config#in-game-config-screen).

---

## Sleep mods

This mod is compatible with the **Sleep Tight** mod.

Mods that let the night pass gradually while players stay in bed, such as **Better Days** and **Seamless Sleep**, work with Midnight Thoughts. The overlay is shown while players sleep, and the summary and the Well Rested effect arrive once the night is over and the sleepers get up.

The rule of thumb: a sleep mod works with Midnight Thoughts if it wakes players at the vanilla morning or later. A mod that wakes players earlier can delay the summary until the vanilla morning.

<Admonition type="warning">
    The overlay, the summary and Well Rested all rely on players actually lying in a bed. Mods that skip the night without anyone sleeping, for example through a vote or a command, do not trigger them.
</Admonition>

---

## Dimension mods

Dimensions from other mods where sleeping is allowed are supported. In a dimension with frozen time, like the Twilight Forest, the summary arrives right after the dimension wakes you up. Details in [when the summary arrives](./summary-screen#other-dimensions).

## Integrations

<Admonition type="info">
    All integrations are optional and only affect the [summary screen](./summary-screen#integrations). Nothing needs to be configured, but each season integration can be switched off in the config.
</Admonition>

| Mod | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **MCA Reborn** | Players who use the MCA skin option get their MCA head on the summary card instead of the vanilla one |
| **Serene Seasons** | Hovering over the day badge on the summary card shows the current season and the day within it |
| **Ecliptic Seasons** | Hovering over the day badge on the summary card shows the current solar term and the day within it |

---

## KubeJS

<Admonition type="tip">
    KubeJS is supported as an **optional** dependency. If it is present, the mod registers a binding and five events. If it is absent, nothing changes and nothing is logged. See [KubeJS](./kubejs).
</Admonition>

---

## Other mods

Because the comfort system is built on **block tags**, blocks from other mods can be made to count towards comfort with a small data pack, with no compatibility patch needed on either side. The [comfort system](./comfort-system) page shows the format.

Well Rested accepts **attributes and potion effects from any mod**. Add their ids to a level in the config and they work like the vanilla ones. See [Well Rested](./well-rested).

Mods that want deeper integration can listen to the [Java API](./java-api) events, including overriding the comfort result entirely.

---

## Client and server

<Admonition type="warning">
    The mod must be installed on **both** the client and the server, in the **same version**. It is not a client-side or server-side only mod. The overlay and summary live on the client, while comfort, statistics and effects live on the server.
</Admonition>
