# Overview

**Midnight Thoughts** expands the sleep experience, transforming sleep from a passive skip mechanic into a meaningful gameplay system.

## What the mod adds

| System | In short |
|---|---|
| [Comfort](./comfort-system) | The blocks around your bed decide how well you sleep |
| [Well Rested](./well-rested) | A five-level buff earned by sleeping in a good place |
| [Sleep overlay](./sleep-overlay) | Dreams, lore and nightmares while the night passes |
| [Summary screen](./summary-screen) | What you did since your last sleep, in four themes |
| [Statistics & MVP](./player-statistics) | Per-player tracking and a nightly winner on servers |
| [Custom content](./custom-facts) | Your own dreams and achievements, no coding needed |

---

## Where to start

<Admonition type="tip" title="Main point">
    Read [comfort system](./comfort-system) first. Almost everything else - the buff level, whether you get nightmares, whether you can sleep at all - follows from it.
</Admonition>

Server owners will want [for servers](./for-servers) and [commands](./commands).

Modpack authors adding their own rules should look at [KubeJS](./kubejs) or the [Java API](./java-api), as well as a customization section for defining your own slides, achievements, and the rest.
